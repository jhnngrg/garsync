GarSync

Send routes planned on Mapy.com to Garmin Connect and your Garmin watch or bike computer in one click.

  1. Plan or open a route on Mapy.com.
  2. In the route panel, click Garmin below Save / Share / Export:
    Save Share Export
    Garmin
  3. Pick your devices and click Send.
Open Mapy.com

You need to be signed in to Garmin Connect in this browser. The route appears on your device after the next sync.
