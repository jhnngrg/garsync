GarSync – Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · extension version 1.0.0

GarSync is a Google Chrome extension that sends a route planned on Mapy.com to your own Garmin Connect account and your Garmin devices. This policy describes the data the extension works with.

In short

  • The extension has no server of its own and sends no data to its author.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Data is processed in your browser and only sent to your own Garmin Connect account when you click Send.

Data the extension works with

  • The Mapy.com route you have open. Its coordinates and name are downloaded as GPX through Mapy.com's export and, when you click Send, uploaded to your Garmin Connect account as a private course.
  • Your Garmin Connect display name. Shown in the dialog only, so you can see which account is connected. It is never stored.
  • Your list of Garmin devices. Loaded so you can choose where to send the course.
  • Your device selection. The IDs of the devices you selected last time are stored locally in the browser (chrome.storage.local) so the dialog can pre-select them. They are removed when you uninstall the extension.

Sign-in

The extension never sees or stores your password. Garmin Connect requests use the session you already have in your browser, just like when you use the Garmin Connect website. They are sent from the extension's background worker, or, if that does not work, from a connect.garmin.com tab the extension opens in the background.

Who receives data

Only the services the extension is built for: Mapy.com (to download the route you have open) and Garmin Connect (to upload it to your account). Their processing is governed by the privacy policies of Seznam.cz and Garmin. Nobody else receives any data.

Permissions

  • storage – remembers your device selection.
  • mapy.com, mapy.cz – adds the Garmin button to the route panel and reads the open route.
  • connect.garmin.com – uploads the course to your account and loads your name and devices.
  • sso.garmin.com – recognises Garmin's sign-in page, so the extension can tell you when you are signed out. Nothing on that page is read or filled in.
  • declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess – gives the extension's background requests to the Garmin Connect API the same headers the Garmin Connect website sends, so no Garmin Connect tab has to be opened. Your normal browsing is not affected.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
